# Arcapush registry

An open, plain-markdown list of the products, AI agents and hackathon builds approved on [Arcapush](https://arcapush.com), one file per category. Each entry has the build's name, its tagline, its free-tier line as the builder stated it, and a link to its Arcapush page.

Only approved, visible, non-archived listings are included, and only public fields. No emails or other private details are ever published here.

## Contributing

The category files are generated from arcapush.com, so they are not edited by hand. To add a build, open a pull request that adds one file under `submissions/` (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
It becomes a draft submission on Arcapush and is reviewed like every other submission. Once approved, it appears here on the next regeneration.

## Quality bar

The same bar Arcapush publishes at [arcapush.com/docs#quality-bar](https://arcapush.com/docs#quality-bar). A build is listed when it has:

- a live primary URL,
- a real logo (not a placeholder),
- at least 1 screenshot,
- a description of 80 words or more.

A listing is archived, and drops out of this registry, when its URL has failed every check for 30 days, or when it is more than 90 days old with no screenshot, no ship-log update and no outbound click in 90 days. Its builder is emailed at least 7 days before, and meeting the bar again brings it straight back.
